HOW TO PAY

During this time, we only accept online payments via CashApp & Venmo. We do not accept credit cards or direct bank transfers at this time, but we will work on adding them in the future.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

1.   Payment is immediately required before any design work begins. Once payment is received, we will begin working on your design. Once the design is complete within the agreed timeframe, we will send you the final design file(s)

2.   Each flyer and business card request has one round of revisions included in the price. Additional revisions or changing the text completely after the design is finished will require purchasing a new design slot. Please make sure to provide clear and detailed feedback to avoid additional charges. (errors and mistakes in the original request will not be counted as revisions, and will be corrected at no additional cost)

3.   To maintain a safe space and keep our focus on building a community, we choose not to take on design requests affiliated with political campaigns or religious organizations at this time.

4.   Printing is not included in the price of any design. We will provide you with the final design file(s) in a high resolution format suitable for printing, but you will be responsible for printing the materials yourself.

While our primary goal is elevating the branding of local businesses with versatile hand crafted designs in a crowded world of generic, AI-generated flyers so common today, we're not limited to that. We will gladly accept design requests for community events, educational institutions, custom templates, and much more.

Do you have a special request in mind or a general suggestion? We would love to hear from you. Please email your request to purpezdesign@gmail.com, and we will get back to you as soon as possible. For any other questions, please contact us.
